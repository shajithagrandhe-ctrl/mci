## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the commanding scale, technical precision, and enduring reliability of global maritime logistics, vessel engineering, and deep-sea industrial operations. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Authoritative & Sovereign:** Projects global scale, maritime safety compliance, and deep institutional legacy without looking antiquated.
- **Architectural & Technical:** Rooted in naval architecture, bridge telemetry, and heavy marine engineering; structural clarity takes precedence over ornamentation.
- **Calm Resilience:** Conveys unwavering stability amidst volatile physical and economic conditions—reassuring shipmasters, port authorities, defense stakeholders, and global enterprise logistics heads.

### Design Movement
The visual language merges **Corporate Modernism** with **Precision Technical Minimalism**. It utilizes structural grid alignment, crisp hairline delineations, oceanic surface layering, and controlled typographic density. Visual weight is derived from deliberate proportion, deep maritime blues, and functional contrast rather than decorative fluff.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in oceanic depth, industrial steel alloys, and stark nautical chart readability.

### Core Swatches
- **Deep Ocean Navy (`#071A2B`):** Deepest anchor shade for high-contrast dark sections, hero panels, and terminal backgrounds.
- **Marine Navy (`#0D2F4F`):** Primary brand anchor. Used for principal navigation bars, key action buttons, and dominant visual weight.
- **Corporate Ocean Blue (`#176B9C`):** Vibrant interactive accent for active states, link emphasis, chart highlights, and focal interactive elements.
- **Refined Steel Blue (`#4E8EAD`):** Supporting technical tertiary color used for secondary telemetry, data visualizations, and contextual callouts.
- **Charcoal Navy (`#142433`):** High-density text neutral that replaces harsh black, providing rich contrast with oceanic warmth.
- **Slate (`#5D6B78`):** Mid-tone functional neutral for secondary copy, icons, subtle borders, and structural metadata.
- **Cool Gray (`#DCE3E8`):** Hairline divider strokes, disabled backgrounds, and grid line definitions.
- **Soft White (`#F7F9FB`):** Standard canvas canvas/body background preventing eye fatigue on data-dense interfaces.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level card backgrounds, elevated overlays, and crisp foreground contrast.

### Palette Execution Rules
- Maintain an authoritative light-mode baseline across dashboards, vessel logs, and corporate portals (`#F7F9FB` canvas with `#FFFFFF` cards).
- Use `#071A2B` and `#0D2F4F` to frame high-impact operational summaries, vessel tracking bridges, or leadership sections.
- Reserve `#176B9C` strictly for forward actions, active routes, and primary focal points.

## Typography

Typography pairs structural authority with utilitarian legibility under all conditions.

### Font Hierarchy
- **Headings & Display (Manrope):** Geometric and authoritative. The tight aperture and structural modern forms project engineering integrity. Headline weights stay within SemiBold (`600`) to ExtraBold (`800`) with negative tracking for commanding presence.
- **Body, Inputs & Technical Meta (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse. Inter delivers flawless optical clarity for vessel coordinates, freight manifest metrics, and multi-column telemetry.
- **Labels & Status Metrics:** Handled in uppercase Inter with wide letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.05em`) to evoke naval instrument panels and shipping classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive grid system provides absolute alignment across complex nautical data dashboards, fleet tracking screens, and executive portals.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, max content width capped at `1440px` for high-density legibility.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
The 8pt spatial baseline determines element placement:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between telemetry tags and icons.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between badge chips, stacked list items, and form label-to-input associations.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal component padding for standard cards and table rows.
- `space-lg` (24px): Padding for complex panels, card interiors, and inter-widget spacing.
- `space-xl` (40px): Segment division between logical operational blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a disciplined, nautical engineering feel, this system eschews heavy decorative shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Crisp Outlines**, and **Subtle Ocean-Tinted Ambient Shading**.

### Architectural Elevation Planes
- **Base Canvas (`#F7F9FB`):** Underlying surface across pages and dashboard canvases.
- **Surface Level 01 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast structural cards, data tables, and input containers. Bounded by a 1px border of `#DCE3E8`.
- **Surface Level 02 (Telemetry & Hover Focus):** Elevated cards, dropdown menus, and popovers carry an ambient, sea-depth-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(7, 26, 43, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(7, 26, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 03 (Modals & Command Dialogs):** Highest focal plane using `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(7, 26, 43, 0.16), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(7, 26, 43, 0.08)` backed by a scrim of `rgba(7, 26, 43, 0.6)`.

### Technical Dividers
Grid divisions and module boundaries use precise hairline strokes (`1px solid #DCE3E8`), evoking navigational charts and engineering diagrams.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** approach, prioritizing disciplined, industrial geometry over playful curves.

### Corner Radius Tokens
- **Standard (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to buttons, inputs, chips, table row selections, and badges. Reflects precision engineering and durable modularity.
- **Large (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to dashboards cards, elevated modals, slide-out fleet panels, and vessel layout cards.
- **Extra Large (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Reserved exclusively for parent structural modules and image masks.
- **Pill Shape:** Strictly prohibited for major containers; permissible only for active vessel status badges (e.g., "Underway", "Moored") to facilitate fast peripheral visual scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0D2F4F` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Subtle hover state to `#176B9C`. Focus ring: 2px offset `#176B9C`. Padding: 10px 20px (Compact: 8px 14px). Border radius: 4px.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with 1px solid `#4E8EAD`, text `#0D2F4F`. Hover creates a subtle background fill of `rgba(78, 142, 173, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#176B9C`, zero border. Hover creates a background fill of `rgba(220, 227, 232, 0.4)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DCE3E8`, text `#142433`, font Inter 14px. Focus state shifts border to `#176B9C` with a 2px `#176B9C` glow at 15% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Structural 16px square or circle with 1px border `#5D6B78`. Selected state fills with `#0D2F4F` displaying a crisp white indicator.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Status Chips:** Flat layout with a 4px radius or pill format.
  - *Active / Operational:* Pale aqua-blue tint (`rgba(23, 107, 156, 0.1)`) with `#176B9C` label.
  - *Warning / Maintenance:* Amber tint with deep slate-brown text.
  - *Anchored / Inactive:* Neutral gray tint (`#DCE3E8`) with `#5D6B78` text.

### Cards & Data Panels
- Encased in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border `#DCE3E8` and 8px border-radius.
- Headers are divided from card content with a 1px stroke of `#DCE3E8`, with section titles set in Manrope SemiBold (`16px`) and actions aligned to the right.

### Tables & Vessel Manifest Lists
- Compact row height (44px) with hairline bottom borders (`#DCE3E8`).
- Header row styled in `#F7F9FB`, with uppercase 11px Inter labels in `#5D6B78` and `0.05em` letter spacing.
- Alternate row striping avoided; row hover states use `rgba(220, 227, 232, 0.35)`.
