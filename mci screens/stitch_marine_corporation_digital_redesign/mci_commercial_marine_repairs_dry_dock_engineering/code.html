Emergency Ballast Tank Plating & Rudder Stock Renewal — M/T Godavari Pride

The Technical Challenge: During active Arabian Sea monsoon transit, the 115,000 DWT Aframax tanker sustained heavy lower strake bottom distortion and internal longitudinal frame buckling following a shallow-water navigational grounding. Rudder torque alarms indicated severe pintle misalignment.

Engineering Resolution: MCI mobilized emergency diving teams for pre-docking laser telemetry within 6 hours. Upon cold-docking in Basin 02, technicians cropped 48 metric tons of damaged bottom shell plating, replaced heavy transverse web frames, and removed the 22-ton rudder assembly for in-situ line boring and re-bedding under DNV GL surveyor oversight.

"Marine Corporation of India's immediate response protocols, around-the-clock shift staffing, and seamless DNV classification coordination saved our charterers over $600,000 in laytime demurrage."

— Fleet Technical Superintendent, Global Tanker Management Pte.
